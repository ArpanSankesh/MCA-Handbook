Attendance budget

Class counts come straight from your timetable — nothing to log by hand. Just set your semester dates once, and optionally note classes you've already missed.

1

Build a buffer early

Attendance is easiest to bank in weeks 1–4, before assignments and mini-projects pile up. Treat every class in this window as non-negotiable.

2

Protect the labs

Web Technology, Advanced Python, DSA Lab and DB Lab run as 2-hour blocks — missing one costs double what a lecture does. Prioritise these when something has to give.

3

Spend the budget, don't chase it

The "can still miss" number already assumes 80%. Once it hits 0 for a subject, every further skip needs a medical certificate, not a guess.
